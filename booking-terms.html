Booking Terms & Conditions

Deposit Policy

A $25 deposit via Zelle is required to secure your appointment. This deposit is deducted from your total service price. Zelle payment instructions are sent privately after booking. Your appointment is not considered secured by the salon until the deposit is verified.

Accepted Payment Methods

We accept Cash and Zelle only. Card payments are not accepted.

Hair Preparation

Please arrive with your hair washed and thoroughly detangled, ready for styling.

Hair Wash & Detangle is available for an additional $25. You must request this service when booking so sufficient time can be arranged.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. Deposits are forfeited for cancellations or changes within 24 hours, or for no-shows.

Additional Charges

Additional charges may apply for extra styling requests, hair length, or other services not included in the original booking. Any additional charges will be discussed with you.

Booking Acknowledgment

By booking an appointment with Crown Beauty Services, you acknowledge these booking terms and conditions.
